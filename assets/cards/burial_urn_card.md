## 2. Picture description

### Urn and pottery

A large terracotta burial urn stands upright on the left side of the composition. Its rounded body narrows into a short neck beneath a broad, thick rim. Warm ochre and peach highlights pick out the rough ceramic surface, while brown shading gives depth to its lower body and hollow interior. Chips, small pits and fine cracks cover the aged pottery.

A large section of the front-right wall is missing, leaving a jagged opening from the shoulder into the body. The surviving wall remains upright, and the broken edges reveal the thickness of the clay. The rim is also chipped and uneven. A faded reddish-brown, branchlike abstract mark appears on the surviving front surface; it is a decorative symbol rather than readable lettering.

### Broken pieces and ground detail

Two large detached pottery pieces lie on the stone floor to the left and in front of the urn. One is a deep curved wall fragment with its dark inner surface visible; the other is a shorter, thick curved shard resting nearby. Small chips, scattered grit and a low patch of dust gather around the fragments and the urn's foot.

The urn and its broken pieces form a grounded arrangement along the left side. Warm light catches the rim, front wall and exposed fracture edges, while the large opening stays deeply shadowed. The dust remains close to the floor, leaving the upper scene and the broad area to the right clear.

### Background and card frame

A shadowed dungeon wall and broad stone arch fill the upper background. Blocky columns frame the arch, and a smaller arched recess with a projecting sill appears behind and to the right of the urn. Uneven floor slabs extend through the lower-left foreground. Charcoal, slate and muted violet masonry fades into a smooth, very dark midnight-blue field toward the reserved text area.

A thin antique-gold border follows the card perimeter. Small gold shard motifs decorate the upper corners, while star accents mark the lower corners. The lower-right ornament remains beyond the reserved text rectangle, near the outside corner. There are no names, numbers, captions, logos or placeholder text in the image.

## 5. Integration notes

- Draw the card first, then draw the game's text and UI elements over the reserved rectangle.
- Use light text, such as ivory or near-white, against the dark field. Choose the font size, line spacing and internal padding for the actual display size.
- Wrap and clip text to the reserved rectangle. The artwork does not define a particular title line, statistic layout or number of description lines.
- Keep the image upright and preserve its full composition. Mirroring would move the quiet text region to the opposite side and require a different overlay layout.
- Use nearest-neighbor/point sampling when retaining crisp pixel edges is the priority. Avoid lossy recompression or texture settings that introduce halos.
- Keep the gold border and corner accents visible. The lower-right ornament sits outside the reserved bounds and should not become part of the text layout.

The image contains artwork only. Overlay text, fonts, interaction behavior and any animated UI effects are supplied by the game.
