## 2. Picture description

### Mage and costume

The mage is a dark-haired, clean-shaven figure wearing a short ivory mantle over a midnight-blue coat. A round gold clasp fastens the mantle, and gold trim and fittings pick out the coat against the dark background. A worn leather belt, dark trousers and sturdy brown boots complete the outfit. The ivory mantle ends around the upper body; the longer garment below the belt is the blue coat.

The face sits in the upper third of the card, slightly left of center, and looks toward the right. The shoulders and upper torso extend into the center of the composition. The body angles toward the lower left, where the coat hem, legs and both boots remain visible. The character is the main focal element.

### Staff and magic

The mage grips a dark staff with gold fittings in the hand on the left side of the image. Its shaft descends along the left side, and its base rests near the boots in the lower-left area. A large gold crescent forms the staff tip in the upper left. A compact violet-and-cyan rift curves around that crescent, with a few small bright accents nearby.

The other hand is raised toward the middle-right area. Warm gold and ivory healing light gathers above the open palm, surrounded by small orbiting arcs and sparks. Both spells stay in the upper and middle parts of the image, keeping the face, hand and staff readable and leaving the lower-right region quiet.

### Background and card frame

A shadowed dungeon arch fills the upper background with low-contrast charcoal and slate masonry. A small amount of stone ground is visible beneath the boots in the lower left. The background becomes smooth, very dark midnight blue toward the reserved text area.

A thin antique-gold border follows the card perimeter. Small celestial star accents decorate the corners. The lower-right corner ornament remains beyond the reserved text rectangle, near the outside corner. There are no names, numbers, captions, logos or placeholder text in the image.

Coordinates use the **top-left image boundary as `(0, 0)`**, with x increasing to the right and y increasing downward. Coordinates are zero-based. The rectangle includes its left and top edges and excludes its right and bottom edges.

Preserve the card's 5:7 aspect ratio and scale the image and text rectangle together. When the displayed card has width `W`, its height is `H = W × 1.4`. For an image drawn at screen position `(imageLeft, imageTop)`:

textX = imageLeft + 450 * scale
textY = imageTop  + 952 * scale
textWidth  = 490 * scale

## 5. Integration notes

- Draw the card first, then draw the game's text and UI elements over the reserved rectangle.
- Use light text, such as ivory or near-white, against the dark field. Choose the font size, line spacing and internal padding for the actual display size.
- Wrap and clip text to the reserved rectangle. The artwork does not define a particular title line, statistic layout or number of description lines.
- Keep the image upright and preserve its full composition. Mirroring would move the quiet text region to the opposite side and require a different overlay layout.
- Use nearest-neighbor/point sampling when retaining crisp pixel edges is the priority. Avoid lossy recompression or texture settings that introduce halos.
- Keep the gold border and corner accents visible. The lower-right ornament sits outside the reserved bounds and should not become part of the text layout.

The image contains artwork only. Overlay text, fonts, interaction behavior and any animated UI effects are supplied by the game.
