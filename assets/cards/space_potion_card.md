## 2. Picture description

### Bottle and contents

A tall glass potion bottle occupies the left side of the card. Its long cylindrical body has a rounded base and curved shoulders that narrow into a slender neck. A cracked ochre-brown cork closes the bottle above a thick, bright glass rim. Pale lavender and ivory reflections run along the glass edges and across the shoulder, making the bottle's rounded volume readable against the dark background.

Vivid violet liquid fills the body up to a distinct elliptical surface beneath the shoulders. Inside it, a luminous cyan ribbon follows a broad, winding S-shaped path, surrounded by small cyan and violet specks. The liquid is darkest at its sides and glows more brightly near the bottom. The bottle carries no label, lettering or numerical markings.

### Suspension and spatial effects

The bottle stands vertically in the composition but floats a short distance above the paving. A dark oval shadow lies directly beneath it, surrounded by violet light on the stones. A soft purple haze and a few bright square motes extend into the space around the glass.

A separate, thin cyan orbit forms a tilted ellipse around the bottle's shoulders and lower neck, rising toward the right. This external ring crosses in front of the glass and continues behind it; it is distinct from the thicker cyan swirl contained in the liquid. The bottle, both cyan effects and the nearby particles remain on the left and middle of the card, leaving the lower-right region quiet.

### Background and card frame

A broad stone dungeon arch curves across the upper background, supported by block-built pillars. Dim charcoal and blue-violet wall masonry shows through the opening. Uneven paving in the lower left catches the potion's purple light, while the background becomes smooth, very dark midnight blue toward the reserved text area.

A thin antique-gold border follows the card perimeter. Small ringed-planet motifs decorate the upper corners, and celestial star accents decorate the lower corners. The lower-right ornament remains beyond the reserved text rectangle, near the outside corner. There are no names, numbers, captions, logos or placeholder text in the image.

textX = imageLeft + 450 * scale

## 5. Integration notes

- Draw the card first, then draw the game's text and UI elements over the reserved rectangle.
- Use light text, such as ivory or near-white, against the dark field. Choose the font size, line spacing and internal padding for the actual display size.
- Wrap and clip text to the reserved rectangle. The artwork does not define a particular title line, statistic layout or number of description lines.
- Keep the image upright and preserve its full composition. Mirroring would move the quiet text region to the opposite side and require a different overlay layout.
- Use nearest-neighbor/point sampling when retaining crisp pixel edges is the priority. Avoid lossy recompression or texture settings that introduce halos.
- Keep the gold border and corner accents visible. The lower-right ornament sits outside the reserved bounds and should not become part of the text layout.

The image contains artwork only. Overlay text, fonts, interaction behavior and any animated UI effects are supplied by the game.
