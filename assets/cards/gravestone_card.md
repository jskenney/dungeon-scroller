## 2. Picture description

### Gravestone and weathered stone

A tall, rounded gravestone stands on the left side of the card. A thick arched border surrounds its recessed front face, and the visible side shows the slab's substantial depth. Cool gray, slate-blue and pale stone pixels describe a coarse, weathered surface crossed by cracks. Yellow-green moss spreads up the lower edges and gathers heavily around the base.

A deep, uneven vertical split divides the stone, with its two upper halves visibly parted. Jagged inner edges interrupt the arch and continue down toward the mossy foot. Smaller horizontal fractures cross the face, and chipped corners and tiny stone fragments reinforce the damaged surface. No name, date or epitaph is carved into the slab.

### Split and cyan glow

A small angular cyan rune glows within the lower-middle portion of the central gap. Its light catches the nearby fracture edges and contrasts with the cold gray stone. The mark is an abstract magical symbol rather than readable lettering.

Several small stone chips appear within and just above the split. The two halves remain upright, and the glowing gap is the scene's main magical accent. Moss, sparse grass, loose stones and dark earth surround the base in the lower-left foreground, keeping the detailed object and ground grouped to the left.

### Background and card frame

An outdoor graveyard stretches behind the gravestone beneath a cloudy night sky. A bright blue-white moon is visible in the upper right, partly crossed by dark cloud bands. A broken stone arch rises at the upper left, while a pointed iron fence and square stone posts cross the distant slope. Smaller headstones, low vegetation and shadowed ground appear farther back on the right. The scene fades into a smooth, very dark midnight-blue field toward the reserved text area.

A thin antique-gold border follows the card perimeter. Small outlined gravestone motifs decorate the upper corners, and star accents decorate the lower corners. The lower-right ornament remains beyond the reserved text rectangle, near the outside corner. There are no names, numbers, captions, logos or placeholder text in the image.

## 5. Integration notes

- Draw the card first, then draw the game's text and UI elements over the reserved rectangle.
- Use light text, such as ivory or near-white, against the dark field. Choose the font size, line spacing and internal padding for the actual display size.
- Wrap and clip text to the reserved rectangle. The artwork does not define a particular title line, statistic layout or number of description lines.
- Keep the image upright and preserve its full composition. Mirroring would move the quiet text region to the opposite side and require a different overlay layout.
- Use nearest-neighbor/point sampling when retaining crisp pixel edges is the priority. Avoid lossy recompression or texture settings that introduce halos.
- Keep the gold border and corner accents visible. The lower-right ornament sits outside the reserved bounds and should not become part of the text layout.

The image contains artwork only. Overlay text, fonts, interaction behavior and any animated UI effects are supplied by the game.
