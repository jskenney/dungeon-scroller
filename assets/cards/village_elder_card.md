## 2. Picture description

### Elder and clothing

The elder is an older woman with silver hair gathered into a low braid that falls down the left side of the pictured figure. Fine lines around her eyes and mouth, a gently attentive gaze and a relaxed expression distinguish her face. She wears a triangular plum wool shawl fastened with a plain round brass brooch over an ankle-length slate-blue dress. A small brown leather pouch hangs at her hip, and sturdy brown leather shoes remain visible below the dress hem.

Her face sits in the upper third of the card, slightly left of center, and looks toward the right. Her upright shoulders, shawl and raised forearm extend into the center of the composition. The skirt folds angle toward the lower left, where both shoes and the end of the walking stick remain visible. The elder is the main focal element.

### Walking stick and gesture

The hand on the left side of the image rests on the top of a plain wooden walking stick. The straight shaft descends along the left side of the figure and plants on the stone ground near her shoes. The entire stick, including its tip, stays inside the card frame.

Her free hand is raised toward the middle-right area in a small palm-up conversational gesture. The open hand is separated from the face and silver braid, keeping the gesture readable at small display sizes. She holds no trade item, and no magical effects appear in the illustration. The stick, dress and gesture leave the lower-right text region clear.

### Background and card frame

A quiet village courtyard fills the upper background with a low slate stone wall, a timber doorway, a tiled cottage roof and patches of greenery in soft afternoon light. A small amount of stone paving is visible beneath the shoes and walking stick in the lower left. The background becomes smooth, very dark midnight blue toward the reserved text area.

A thin antique-gold border follows the card perimeter. Small woven knot motifs decorate the upper corners, echoing the charms the elder sells. A small gold leaf ornament remains beyond the reserved text rectangle, near the lower-right outside corner. There are no names, titles, dialogue, statistics, logos or placeholder text in the image.

textX = imageLeft + 450 * scale
textY = imageTop  + 952 * scale
textWidth  = 490 * scale

## 5. Integration notes

- Draw the card first, then draw the game's text and UI elements over the reserved rectangle.
- Use light text, such as ivory or near-white, against the dark field. Choose the font size, line spacing and internal padding for the actual display size.
- Wrap and clip text to the reserved rectangle. The artwork does not define a particular title line, statistic layout or number of description lines.
- Keep the image upright and preserve its full composition. Mirroring would move the quiet text region to the opposite side and require a different overlay layout.
- Use nearest-neighbor/point sampling when retaining crisp pixel edges is the priority. Avoid lossy recompression or texture settings that introduce halos.
- Keep the gold border and corner accents visible. The lower-right ornament sits outside the reserved bounds and should not become part of the text layout.

The image contains artwork only. Overlay text, fonts, interaction behavior and any animated UI effects are supplied by the game.
